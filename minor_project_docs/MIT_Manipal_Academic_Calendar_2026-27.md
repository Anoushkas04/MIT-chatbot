# Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT), Manipal — Academic Calendar 2026-27

Institution: Manipal Institute of Technology, Manipal (a constituent unit of MAHE, Manipal)
Academic year: 2026-27
Odd Semester: July – December 2026
Even Semester: January – May 2027 (with makeup exams and next re-registration through July 2027)

Date format used below: YYYY-MM-DD (Weekday) — Event. Each line is self-contained.
Only dates with an event are listed. All Sundays are holidays and are not listed unless an event falls on them.

## General Notes
- Events marked with * are a holiday only for students (e.g., Holi*, Bakrid*).
- Events marked with ~ are subject to change as per MAHE Circular (e.g., Ramzan~, Bakrid~).
- Odd semester Mid-term Re-test will be scheduled tentatively from 2026-10-19 to 2026-11-06.
- Even semester Mid-term Re-test will be scheduled tentatively from 2027-03-19 to 2027-04-19.
